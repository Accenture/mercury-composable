## Why these five swept when the previous review swept zero

The prior review kept its five completed threads on the stated grounds that they were "closed within
the window." Applying that rule literally here would have kept these five too — they were also
closed yesterday.

The distinction that actually matters is **which of the two clocks has run out**. Those five were
closed *and recently referenced* (low sslu): live work, freshly finished, context still worth having
in the live layer. These five were closed *and unreferenced for 114–189 sessions*: they faded months
before the audit got around to ticking their boxes. Closing a long-dormant thread does not make it
recent — it only records that it is done.

So "keep recently-completed threads for context" means recently *worked on*, not recently
*check-marked*. Worth writing down because the box-ticking date is the tempting proxy and it is the
wrong one — the same class of error as using git dates for thread staleness, which the upstream team
corrected us on yesterday.

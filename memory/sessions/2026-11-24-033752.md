## Context

Issue #664 asks for four simple plugins in the date family. `f:addDays` and `f:daysBetween` (4.12.26) compute in days;
a month is not a fixed number of days, so a rule stated in months or years was a calendar algorithm written out in
`graph.math` statements: 14 `floor` calls to add months to one date, 18 to count the months between two.

## The review, before any change

- **The issue's facts hold.** Its nine edge-case rows reproduce on the machine's OpenJDK 27.0.1. The date plugins and
  the shared vectors had not changed since the commit the issue read, and the vector file was byte-identical in the two
  repositories.
- **The Rust library agrees, measured.** jiff 1.0.1 gives `java.time`'s answer on every row, and its month count equals
  the JDK's formula (`(packed(b) - packed(a)) / 32`, truncated toward zero) on all 267,289 pairs of dates from 2027-11-01
  to 2029-03-31, both orders. So the twin can call the library, and "in either order" is well defined: the absolute
  value is the same both ways.
- **What the issue left out, raised to the maintainer:** a count taken after an addition that ended on a shorter month
  is one short (`2027-01-31` and one month give `2027-02-28`, 0 whole months later); the last day of a month does not
  stay a last day (`2027-02-28` and one month give `2027-03-28`); each unit needs its own bound; the vectors' code
  `bad-days` is named for days; and the list of files omitted the plugin catalog, the command reference, the agent
  guide's checklist, the Playground help page with its bundle, the gate test and a graph fixture.

## Decisions

- **The maintainer's:** start the Java side with the issue's proposed rules - the four plugins, an absolute
  `monthsBetween` and `yearsBetween` in either order as `daysBetween` is, whole months and years only.
- **Made here, stated in the guides, open to the maintainer's review:**
  - The plugins call `LocalDate.plusMonths`, `plusYears` and `ChronoUnit.MONTHS` / `YEARS.between`; nothing is
    re-implemented.
  - The months and the years are read by the rule that reads the days (`DatePluginUtils.count`), each bounded by the
    widest writable span before any arithmetic: 119,999 months, 9,999 years. The message names the unit: "The months
    of addMonths must be a whole number".
  - Three rules are declared in the guides, with a vector each, and none is engineered around: a day the resulting
    month lacks becomes that month's last day and no other date moves; a month is complete when the day of the month
    is reached, a year when the month and day are; after an addition that ended on a shorter month the count is one
    short of the number added. No end-of-month option, no signed variant.
  - The vectors gain the codes `bad-months` and `bad-years`; `bad-days` is matched on "The days of", so the three
    codes no longer share one pattern. The file's version stays 1, as every shared vector file's does.

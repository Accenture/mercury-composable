**The finding worth declining loudly:** try-with-resources on `ElasticQueue`/`FileElasticStore`
(5 of the 19) would be *wrong*, not merely noise. `FileElasticStore.close()` only calls
`resetCounter()` — a **logical** close the tests assert on directly — while the real teardown is
`destroy()` (close + purge). The suggestion would call the wrong method for cleanup and inject an
automatic `close()` into the very tests whose subject is close/reuse semantics. A reviewer applying
static-analysis suggestions mechanically would have broken three tests' meaning while keeping them
green.

**Declined as noise:** empty `while` drain bodies (already idiomatic with a comment),

(`vthread`, `tmpfs`), and two extract-method suggestions.

## Decision

Two configuration switches that answer the same question invite a reader - an AI agent first - to combine them. When
one mechanism is sufficient (the claim for the instance, the environment store for the site), the other is retired,
not documented around. Eric asked for the ADR to supersede ADR-0030 ("please write the ADR for the retirement to
supercede ADR-0030"): **ADR-0037** (second commit on the PR branch, `755bca3fd`) states one instance by the claim and one
site by the environment store in a single record, carries ADR-0030's active-environment decision forward unchanged,
retires `leader.election` (ignored with a warning, never refused - the examples shipped the key as `false`, so field
cron files carry it), and names the generalizing rule. ADR-0030 is marked Superseded by ADR-0037; its `formalizes:`
fact `scheduler-active-environment-store` is archived (2026-Q4) and stays so, its substance carried in the new entry's
"one site" clause. `scheduler-resolver-conditional-claim` is ADR-0037's `formalizes:` fact and now carries the
`(ADR-0037)` tag. The guide's See-also and the CHANGELOG's Removed 1 point at ADR-0037; `ai-contract-provider` passes
with the enlarged ledger (the snapshot packages `ADR.md`).

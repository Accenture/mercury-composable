## Lessons

- A lineage check must be positive about every hop: a tree without orphans can still be missing legs. The check is "every
  record's parent is owned" plus "every hop left a record".
- When one side's record is suppressed by a rule, the rule stands only with the other side's record in place; the packs had
  copied the engines' suppression without the engines' edge records.
- The packs' RPC reply must carry the responder's span, or an inbox record for a pack route has nothing the forwarder keeps.

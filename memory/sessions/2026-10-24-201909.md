**Title:** `f:addDays` and `f:daysBetween` - two date plugins the field suggested, on the Java engine (branch and PR)

## Context

Eric relayed a field suggestion: two simple plugins in the event-script-engine, available to the MiniGraph Playground.
`f:addDays(date, days)` returns the date `days` later as `YYYY-MM-DD` (`2027-01-15`, 15 gives `2027-01-30`), and
`f:daysBetween(from, to)` the whole days between two dates as an integer (`2027-04-10` to `2027-05-11` gives 31). Dates are
ISO-8601 `YYYY-MM-DD` only, no time and no time zone; the start date is excluded and the end date included, so the same date
twice gives 0 and two consecutive days give 1.

## Decisions

- **`daysBetween` is symmetric (Eric's ruling, mid-session):** `(from, to)` and `(to, from)` give the same answer, so it
  returns the absolute count and is never negative. The earlier date is the excluded one.
- **Made here, stated in the docs, open to Eric's review:**
  - A date is text that is exactly `YYYY-MM-DD` and a real day of the proleptic Gregorian calendar (`2027-02-29` and
    `2100-02-29` are refused, `2028-02-29` is accepted); surrounding spaces, a time, a zone suffix, a single-digit month and a
    date-like number are refused by name.
  - A result must be writable as `YYYY-MM-DD`, so it falls within 0000-01-01 to 9999-12-31; the days are bounded by that span
    (3,652,424) before any date arithmetic, so no overflow is possible.
  - The days are a whole number read through `DecimalPluginUtils.operand`: an integer, canonical numeric text (`"15"`,
    `"-1"`) or a floating-point number whose value is whole (`15.0`); a fraction, a word, a boolean, a list and NaN are refused.
    A negative number gives an earlier date.
  - **A null argument is refused**, like the arithmetic and decimal families, not answered with null. Loosening it later to
    null-in, null-out would break no graph; tightening it later would.
  - `daysBetween` returns an `Integer` (at most 3,652,424), so `f:eq(model.days, int(31))` compares equal.

## Follow-ups for Eric (not done)

- The guide's *Schema cache* note and `ManagedCacheSchemaRegistryClient`'s javadoc say the serdes look schemas up through its
  `getSchemaById`; in Confluent 8.3.2, `AbstractKafkaSchemaSerDe.getSchemaBySchemaId` (the serde's lookup by id) calls
  `getSchemaBySubjectAndId` on the client, which Confluent's own in-memory cache serves, so the `ManagedCache` TTL likely applies
  to the codec's own lookups only. Read from bytecode, not measured.
- Two imprecisions in the merged guide's outage paragraph, found while porting it to Rust (Confluent 8.3.2's sources:
  `RestService.isRestClientExceptionRetriable`, `RetryExecutor`): "HTTP 408, 429 or 5xx" should read 408, 429, 500, 502,
  503 or 504 (501 and 505 are not retried); and "a publish ... fails its flow, which the adapter retries and then
  dead-letters" holds only for a flow the adapter started - a REST-started flow fails back to its caller. The Rust guide
  states both precisely.

engines): every statement command resolves `{dynamic variables}`** — `NEXT:`,
`THEN:`/`ELSE:` targets, `RESET:` entries, `DELAY:` values:

  targets stay deliberately literal under the reserved-metadata guard.

  skipped, jump target fails loudly ("Next node 'null' does not exist").

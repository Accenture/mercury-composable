### Root cause, and the durable fix

`scripts/check-doc-canon.py` touched llms.txt for **retired terminology only** — it never
checked that a guide *has* an entry. So the canon page could assert llms.txt "is kept current"
while nothing enforced it, and any new guide shipped invisible to the guide-first lookup rule.
Added **check 7** (every guide page and DSL catalog must be listed), updated the canon page's
own Enforcement section and its llms.txt bullet, and **verified the check bites** — removed one
entry, confirmed exit 1 naming the file, restored, confirmed exit 0. A gate that only ever
passes is worthless.

Entry sizing was calibrated per section: the Reference block runs 143-270 chars, far terser than
Operate & integrate, so the two Reference entries were trimmed twice to sit in that band.

## Durable lesson

**A "complete X" audit must match the shape of X.** I reported two missing guides from a
top-level `ls` of a tree that is two levels deep; the real number was nine. When claiming
completeness over a tree, walk it recursively and say which tree was walked. The under-count
was mine and I surfaced it before it became someone's planning input.

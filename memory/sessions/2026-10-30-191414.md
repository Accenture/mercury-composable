## What was done

- **The cache evaluation** (no code change; Eric's ruling is [[minimalist-kafka-schema-cache-ruling]]). Read from the
  Confluent 8.3.2 sources jars in `~/.m2` (`CachedSchemaRegistryClient`, `AbstractKafkaSchemaSerDe`, the JSON and Avro
  serializers and deserializers), then measured with a throwaway JUnit probe, deleted afterwards and never committed: a JDK
  `HttpServer` registry counting requests by URI, and the real `SchemaCodec` built under the prefix `schema.probe` with a 1 s
  `cache.ttl`. Per phase:
  - serialize ×3: one `GET /schemas/ids/1?subject=` (Mercury's `JsonSchemaSerde.jsonSchemaById`, through the override) and
    one `GET /schemas/ids/1?subject=probe-value` (Confluent's serializer, `lookupSchemaEntityBySubjectAndId`), plus a
    Confluent 8 `GET /associations/...` per serde instance;
  - decode ×3: no id request;
  - resolve `latest` ×3 and pinned `1` ×3: one request each;
  - after the 1 s ManagedCache TTL, a decode and a serialize: **no request**; a `latest` resolve: **no request** (Confluent's
    `latestVersionCache`, 60 s);
  - Confluent alone, `getSchemaMetadata(subject, 1)` ×3: **three requests**; `getLatestSchemaMetadata` ×3: none; by id with
    and without a subject ×3: none.

  So the id cache never saves a request, the `latest` entry delays a new version from 60 s to 30 min, and the pinned-version
  cache saves one request per publish.
- **The guide's retry statuses** (branch `docs/minimalist-kafka-registry-retry-statuses`): "HTTP 408, 429 or 5xx" now reads
  408, 429, 500, 502, 503 or 504, and the paragraph says any other answer fails at once (501, 505, a not-found id, a refused
  credential), per `RestService.isRestClientExceptionRetriable`. The Unreleased Documentation 1 entry said Confluent "retries a

## The asymmetry worth remembering

`test-secret` is a recognised placeholder token; `inherit-secret` is not. The guard was **right** to
flag the second — a reader cannot tell that value is fake, and "obviously a dummy to the author" is
not a property a scanner can read. Naming a test credential `test-*` is therefore not cosmetic; it
is what makes it legible as fake to tooling and to the next reader.

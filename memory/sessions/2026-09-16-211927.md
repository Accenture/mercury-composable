durable home**. The pending Dynatrace confirmation of the two v4.12.11 field-acceptance traces was
recorded *only* inside continuity's `latest_release` entry. That entry is **rewritten wholesale at
every release**, so the commitment would have evaporated at v4.12.12 with nobody noticing: the report
would have kept saying "pending" forever, and the trace ids needed to check it would have been gone
from the live layer.

Now a thread of its own, carrying both trace ids, the field to read (`OTel scope version` = 4.12.11),
and the instruction to *update* Scenario 6 rather than rewrite it.

**Generalizable:** `latest_release` is a **status field, not a tracker**. Anything in it that is a
commitment rather than a description of the shipped release needs its own thread, because that field
has a scheduled overwrite. Worth checking at each review: does `latest_release` contain any sentence
describing work that has not happened yet?

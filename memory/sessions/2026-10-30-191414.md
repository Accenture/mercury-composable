    one `GET /schemas/ids/1?subject=probe-value` (Confluent's serializer, `lookupSchemaEntityBySubjectAndId`), plus a

  - decode ×3: no id request;
  - resolve `latest` ×3 and pinned `1` ×3: one request each;
  - after the 1 s ManagedCache TTL, a decode and a serialize: **no request**; a `latest` resolve: **no request** (Confluent's
    `latestVersionCache`, 60 s);
  - Confluent alone, `getSchemaMetadata(subject, 1)` ×3: **three requests**; `getLatestSchemaMetadata` ×3: none; by id with
    and without a subject ×3: none.

  So the id cache never saves a request, the `latest` entry delays a new version from 60 s to 30 min, and the pinned-version
  cache saves one request per publish.

## Decisions

- The assumed step is a synchronous request from the walker, not a mode of `graph.task`: no reply disambiguation on the walker's
  correlation id and no per-instance state beyond the arming bit, while the request/response shape, the staging under `root.*` and the
  error path are exactly a task node's. The mismatch warning lives in the gate (deploy and pack), never on the console. Nothing is
  cached across requests: compiling a schema is a map walk cheaper than the event hop.
